# Services and prices

## General service
$65. Gears and brakes adjusted, chain cleaned and oiled, wheels checked, bolts tightened. Most
are done in 2 working days.

## Full service
$120. Everything in the general service, plus the bike stripped, cleaned and rebuilt, with new
cables where needed. Usually 3 working days. Parts are extra.

## Punctures
$15 for the repair, plus $8 for a new tube if the old one cannot be patched. Done the same day
if you drop the bike in before noon.

## Brakes
Hydraulic brake bleed: $35 per brake. Brake pad fitting: $12 per brake, plus the pads.

## Wheels
Wheel truing from $20. Custom wheel builds are priced per build and need a 50% deposit, which we
refund in full until we order the parts.

## E-bikes and tubeless tyres
We service e-bikes, but not the motor or battery, which go back to the maker. Tubeless tyre setup
is $30 per wheel, sealant included.
