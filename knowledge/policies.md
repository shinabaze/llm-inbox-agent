# Policies

## Workmanship guarantee
If a repair or service we did goes wrong within 30 days (brakes, gears, anything we worked on),
bring the bike back and we fix it free.

## Refunds and billing mistakes
Refunds and billing mistakes are handled by the owner, Sam, who replies within 1 working day.
Nobody else at the shop can promise a refund.

## Sponsorship and donations
We sponsor two local events a year and give service vouchers to school and charity rides. Sam
reads every request and answers within a week.

## Suppliers
We buy parts through our existing trade accounts. Sam reads sales emails when he can.
