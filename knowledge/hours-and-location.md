# Hours and location

## Opening hours
Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm. Saturday 9am to 4pm. Closed on Sunday and on public holidays.

## Where we are
14 Kettle Lane, next to the station car park. You can park outside the shop for 30 minutes, and
there is a covered bike rack by the door.

## Dropping off and collecting
Drop a bike off any time we are open. No appointment is needed for a quick look. We text you when
it is ready, and anyone you name can collect it. Bikes left more than 14 days after we text you
are charged a storage fee of $5 for each day.
